ABOUT US

NOW THAT ITS OVER RECORDS is an independent record label focused on experimental and underground music. We believe in artist autonomy, fair deals, and putting the music first. Founded in 2020, we operate as a collective — no middlemen, no gatekeepers.

We work with artists across electronic, ambient, noise, and beyond. Our approach is simple: transparent splits, non-exclusive agreements, and a genuine commitment to getting your music in front of the right ears.
